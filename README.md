# Daily Spend

A simple app for keeping track of what you spend each day.

**Open it here: <https://bfirst786.github.io/daily-spend/>**

It's free, needs no account, and works without an internet connection once installed.
Everything you enter stays on your phone. Nobody else can see it, including the person
who sent you the link.

## Install it on your phone

Install takes about 30 seconds. Afterwards it has its own icon and opens full screen
like any other app.

### iPhone or iPad

1. Open the link above in **Safari**. Other browsers on iPhone can't install apps.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up), at the bottom of the screen.
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then tap **Add**.
4. Open **Daily Spend** from your home screen.

### Android

1. Open the link above in **Chrome**.
2. Tap **Install** when the app offers it, or tap the **⋮** menu and choose
   **Install app** (on some phones it says **Add to Home screen**).
3. Open **Daily Spend** from your home screen or app drawer.

On a computer, you can just use the link in your browser. Chrome and Edge also show an
install icon in the address bar.

## Using it

- **Set your daily budget** under Settings, at the bottom. The bar at the top turns
  yellow when you're close to it and red when you're over.
- **Add a purchase:** type the amount (the decimal point stays put, so 1-2-5-0 is
  $12.50), pick a category, add a note if you want, and tap **Add**.
- **Say it:** tap **Say it**, then say something like "12.50 lunch at Chipotle".
  If your phone doesn't listen right away, tap the box and use the microphone on
  your keyboard.
- **Quick log:** tap **Quick log** for a big full-screen keypad, good for adding
  several purchases in a row. Tap **Done** or swipe back to return. On Android you
  can also press and hold the app icon and choose **Quick log**.
- **Fix a purchase:** tap it to change the amount, category, note, date or time.
- **Archive** (box icon) keeps a purchase but leaves it out of your totals. Use it for
  returns or things you got paid back for. **Delete** (trash icon) removes it for good.
- **Other days:** use the arrows at the top, tap a bar in the 14-day chart, or tap
  **Show earlier days** under Purchases.

## Keep a backup

Your purchases live only on this phone, inside this app. They are deleted if you
delete the app, clear your browser's website data, or lose the phone.

- Under **Backup**, tap **Save backup** every so often and keep the file somewhere safe
  (Files, Google Drive, or email it to yourself).
- To move to a new phone, install the app there, tap **Restore backup** and pick that file.

## Questions

**Do I need to update it?** No. When you open it with an internet connection, you get
the latest version automatically.

**Does it cost anything or show ads?** No.

**Is my data sent anywhere?** No. The app has no server and no account. The only copy
is on your phone, plus any backups you save.
